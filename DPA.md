# Data Processing Agreement (DPA)

*Last updated September 28th, 2022*[cite: 82]

THIS DATA PROCESSING AGREEMENT (“DPA”) IS ENTERED BETWEEN DATA SELLER AS STATED IN THE DATA COMMERCE CLOUD AGREEMENT (“DCCA”) AS A CONTROLLER AND DATARADE AS A PROCESSOR CONCERNING THE PROCESSING OF PERSONAL DATA UNDER THE DCCA[cite: 82]. THE TERMS AND CONDITIONS OF THIS DPA WILL BE LEGALLY BINDING ON THE PARTIES UPON THE EFFECTIVE DATE[cite: 82]. UNLESS STATED TO THE CONTRARY HEREINAFTER, THE DEFINITIONS OF THE DCCA SHALL APPLY[cite: 82]. FOR THE AVOIDANCE OF DOUBT, IN CASE OF A CONFLICT BETWEEN THE PROVISIONS OF THIS DPA AND THE DCCA, THE DPA SHALL PREVAIL[cite: 82].

## 1. General Responsibilities of the Parties

* **Controller-Processor Relationship:** Providing Data Seller with functionality of the Platform requires the processing of Data Seller’s Content[cite: 82]. If and inasmuch as such Content consists of or contains personal data within the meaning of Data Protection Laws, Datarade will act as a processor regarding such data, whereas Data Seller remains the controller regarding such data[cite: 82].
* **Data Seller’s Responsibilities as a Controller:** By means of the Platform, Data Seller submits and processes its own Content, largely controls the upload, and directly handles the use of any such Content that has been submitted to Datarade[cite: 82]. Data Seller agrees and understands that Datarade will not monitor Content or Data Seller’s use of any such Content, unless Data Seller submits an explicit written request to Datarade to access Content[cite: 82]. In any other case, only Data Seller knows which data comprise the Content[cite: 82]. It is, therefore, the sole responsibility and liability of Data Seller to ensure that Content is collected and transmitted to Datarade in compliance with applicable Data Protection Laws and, in particular, (a) to always observe the principles relating to processing of personal data including, without limitation, the principles of purpose limitation and data minimization; (b) to have a legal basis for its processing; and (c) to properly inform data subjects of the collection and processing of their personal data[cite: 82].
* **Datarade’s Responsibilities as a Processor:** Acting as a processor, Datarade will process personal data on Data Seller’s behalf only in accordance with the provisions of this DPA and the documented instructions received from Data Seller[cite: 82].

## 2. Details of the Processing

* **Nature, Purpose and Subject Matter:** Datarade will process personal data to provide the Platform as further specified in the DCCA[cite: 82].
* **Duration:** Valid for the duration of the DCCA term and automatically expires upon deletion of all processed personal data[cite: 82].
* **Categories of Data Subjects:** Data Seller’s employees, representatives, external consultants, contractors, advisors, partners, agents, or other individuals whose personal data is submitted to Datarade[cite: 82].
* **Types of Personal Data:** Contact data, demographic data, and advanced demographic attributes[cite: 82].
